## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is PocketKnife Vault free?**
Yes — completely free with no ads and no subscription required.

**Is internet access required to use PocketKnife Vault?**
No - PocketKnife Vault does not require an internet connection.

**Is my username and password data backed up?**
PocketKnife Vault explicitly excludes its database from iCloud and iTunes backups. Your vault data does not travel to Apple's servers via backup services.

**Does PocketKnife Vault use any third-party services?**
PocketKnife Vault does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or external services of any kind.

## Privacy
PocketKnife Vault does not collect or store any personal data. All your saved data is stored locally on your device and never shared.
