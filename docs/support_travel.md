## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is PocketKnife Travel free?**
Yes — completely free with no ads and no subscription required.

**Is internet access required to use PocketKnife Travel?**
Almost all features work completely offline. The Currency Converter requires 
an internet connection on first use to fetch live exchange rates — after that, 
rates are cached on your device for up to 6 hours and work without a connection. 
All other features — trip planning, itinerary, packing lists, budget tracking, 
time zones, saved links, and maps — work without any internet connection.

**Is my trip data backed up?**
All your data is stored locally on your device. We recommend using your device's 
built-in backup (iCloud) to protect your trip data. Cloud sync within the app 
is planned for a future version.

**Can I use PocketKnife Travel for domestic US trips?**
Absolutely. The app works for any trip — international or domestic. 
Destinations support both countries and US states.

## Privacy
PocketKnife Travel does not collect or store any personal data. All your saved 
data — trips, itineraries, packing lists, expenses, and saved links — is stored 
locally on your device and never shared. The only network request the app makes 
is to fetch currency exchange rates from Frankfurter (European Central Bank data).
