# Privacy Policy — PocketKnife Budget
Last updated: May 2026

## PocketKnife Budget is a free, no-account, fully offline personal budgeting app for iOS.  
 - Track income, expenses, and your check register without giving up your data.
 - Completely Free. No Ads. No Upsells.
 - PocketKnife Budget is free to download and use — every feature, forever. No premium tiers, no ads, and nothing to unlock.

## Data Collection
 - PocketKnife Budget does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server. 
 - The developer has no access to your data, your device, or your identity at any time.

## Third-Party Services
 - PocketKnife Budget does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or external services of any kind.

## Children
 - PocketKnife Budget does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy
 - Any future changes to this policy will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this document. Substantive changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
