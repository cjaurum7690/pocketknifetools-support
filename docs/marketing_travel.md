# Privacy Policy for PocketKnife Travel
Last updated: May 10, 2026

PocketKnife Travel ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data — by not collecting it.

## 1. No Data Collection
PocketKnife Travel does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.
Specifically:
- We do not track usage behavior, device information, or user identity.
- We do not use analytics, advertising, or cloud-based user accounts.
- All data you enter (trips, itineraries, packing lists, expenses, saved links)
  is stored exclusively on your device using local storage and never leaves it.

## 2. Third-Party Services
The Currency Converter feature makes network requests to Frankfurter
(api.frankfurter.dev), a free exchange rate API powered by European Central
Bank data. No account is required and no user-identifiable information is ever
sent — requests contain only the currency codes being converted. Fetched rates
are cached locally on your device for up to 6 hours to minimize network usage
and support offline operation.

The Maps feature uses Apple's native Maps app via a standard deep link. No
data is transmitted to Apple beyond what Apple Maps itself handles when you
choose to open it.

## 3. Location Data
PocketKnife Travel does not request access to your device's location and never
transmits location data to us or any third party.

## 4. Children's Privacy
PocketKnife Travel is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly
collect personal data from anyone.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in functionality or legal
requirements. Updates will be posted within the App and/or on our support page.
