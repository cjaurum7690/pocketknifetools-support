# PocketKnife Travel
PocketKnife Travel is a lightweight trip planner designed to help travelers organize, track, and manage every detail of their journeys — all stored privately on their device.

## Features
- Trip Planning & Itinerary Builder
- Packing Lists with Built-in Templates
- Budget & Expense Tracker
- Live Currency Converter
- Time Zone Comparison
- Saved Links & Confirmation Numbers
- Apple Maps Integration
