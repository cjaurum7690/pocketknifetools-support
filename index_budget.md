# PocketKnife Budget
PocketKnife Budget is a free, no-account, fully offline personal budgeting app for iOS.  Track income, expenses, and your check register without giving up your data.

## Features
No Account. No Nonsense.
 - Download and open. That’s it. No email, no password, no cloud sync required. PocketKnife Budget starts working the moment you launch it.

Your Data Lives on Your Device
 - Every number you enter stays on your phone. There’s no server, no sync service, and no company looking at your financial data. Ever.

A Check Register in Your Pocket
 - The Ledger gives you an old-school check register experience — set a starting balance, log credits and debits, and watch your running balance update instantly. Mark entries cleared when they post.

Recurring Expenses That Actually Work
 - Add a subscription or bill once and choose its schedule: weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, or annual. It appears automatically in every applicable month until you delete it.

Weekly Budget Tracking
 - Assign expenses to a week number and set a weekly spending limit. The app shows a bar for each week — green when you’re on track, yellow when you’re close, red when you’re over.

Charts That Tell the Whole Story
 - Four interactive charts give you the full picture: a bar chart of monthly income vs. expenses, a donut chart of spending by category (tap any slice for transactions), a monthly net trend line, and a weekly budget bar chart.

Completely Free. No Ads. No Upsells.
 - PocketKnife Budget is free to download and use — every feature, forever. No premium tiers, no ads, and nothing to unlock.
